Systems

The kinds of device AeroCore runs on. Each has its own plugin set, its own config and its own version line. A bundle names its system in the system field its build stamps into every manifest, and a node is placed by the version it reports.

Accounts

Accounts live in Keycloak, so one created here also signs in to AeroCore and aerotunnel. A new account gets the customer role — the lowest privilege level — and its temporary password must be changed at first sign-in. Anything beyond that is granted in Keycloak, deliberately.
